Live Apprenticeship Marketplace

Connecting apprenticeship employers with students who are ready to work

UCAS is a live marketplace that helps business owners find apprentices and helps students discover opportunities they did not know existed.

See the deck Explore the platform

The problem

Opportunities and talent keep missing each other

Most students never consider an apprenticeship because they do not know what is available. Most employers who offer apprenticeships struggle to reach those students directly. The result is a marketplace where willing candidates and real roles never connect.

The fix

UCAS – a direct marketplace for apprenticeships

We built UCAS to close that gap, a marketplace where apprenticeship employers and students find each other without intermediaries. The platform is live and already connecting both sides, with a focus on scaling that connection across more regions and sectors.

How UCAS works

  1. 1

    Create Profile

    Students and employers set up profiles with the details that matter for matching: sector, location, level, and availability.

  2. 2

    Browse or Post

    Students explore apprenticeship listings while employers publish roles with structured detail that goes live immediately.

  3. 3

    Connect and Apply

    Direct messaging allows both sides to ask questions before applying, leading to more submitted applications and better match quality.

  4. 4

    Track Progress

    Employers manage applicants through the pipeline and students see their status across all applications in one view.

What the platform does

  1. 01

    Apprentice Discovery

    Students browse real apprenticeship openings from verified employers, filtered by sector, location, and level. Each listing shows what the role involves, what qualifications are expected, and what the pay looks like.

  2. 02

    Employer Listings

    Business owners post apprenticeship roles with structured details: duration, training provider, wage, and career path. Listings go live quickly and reach students who are actively searching for apprenticeship opportunities.

  3. 03

    Direct Messaging

    This helps ensure questions are answered before an application is submitted, reducing unsubmitted applications and accelerating placements for both sides.

  4. 04

    Application Tracking

    Employers see where each applicant sits in the pipeline, from first enquiry to signed contract. Students get visibility on their status across every application without chasing emails or phone calls.

  5. 05

    Analytics Dashboard

    Employers track listing performance: views, enquiries, and conversion to placement. Students see which roles they have applied to and where they stand, all in one place.

Inside the platform

Why we built UCAS

I kept meeting talented students who had never been shown what an apprenticeship could actually look like, and employers who were quietly offering roles with no route to reach those students. We built UCAS to put both sides in the same room, a live marketplace where the apprenticeship pathway is the default, not an afterthought. The platform is live today because the gap was real and the fix was straightforward: structured listings, direct messaging, and a single tracked pipeline that respects both sides' time.

Frequently asked questions

Is UCAS live?

Yes. The marketplace is launched and currently connecting students with apprenticeship employers.

Who uses the platform?

Students looking for apprenticeship opportunities and business owners who want to hire apprentices. Both sides interact directly within the platform.

How do employers post a role?

Employers create a listing through the dashboard, add structured details about the apprenticeship, and publish it to reach students who are actively searching.

Is there a cost to students?

No. Students use the platform at no cost. Employers pay to post and promote listings.

How is UCAS different from existing job boards?

General job boards do not target students specifically and rarely include structured apprenticeship detail. UCAS is built for the apprenticeship pathway, with fields that matter to students and employers in that context.

What types of apprenticeships are listed?

Roles span sectors from engineering and construction to digital and business admin, at levels from intermediate to degree apprenticeships.

How do you verify employers?

We check employer details before listings go live to ensure students are applying to legitimate apprenticeship opportunities.

What is the business model?

Employers pay to post and promote listings. Students access the platform for free. Premium promotion and analytics features are available to employers at higher tiers.
